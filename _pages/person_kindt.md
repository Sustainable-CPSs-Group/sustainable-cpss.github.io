**Dr.-Ing. Philipp H. Kindt**
Postdoctoral Researcher, Chair of Cyber-Physical Systems in Production Engineering,
TUM School of Engineering and Design

Philipp Kindt works on low-power wireless connectivity for cyber-physical systems and the Internet
of Things — neighbor discovery, connection scheduling and the energy behaviour of wireless
protocols in resource-constrained devices. His position in the Focus Group is funded by the TUM
Institute for Advanced Study.
