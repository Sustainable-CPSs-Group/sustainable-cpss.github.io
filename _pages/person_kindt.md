## TUM-IAS Postdoctoral Researchers

**Dr.-Ing. Philipp H. Kindt**  
Postdoctoral Researcher, Chair of Cyber-Physical Systems in Production Engineering,
TUM School of Engineering and Design

Philipp Kindt works on low-power wireless connectivity for cyber-physical systems and the Internet
of Things — neighbor discovery, connection scheduling and the energy behaviour of wireless
protocols in resource-constrained devices.

In the Focus Group he works on what it takes for a sensing node to run without a battery at all. He
is the lead author of [_Predictive Maintenance without
Maintenance_](/blog/2026/predictive-maintenance-without-maintenance/), and a co-author on our work
on deterministic BLE transports. His position is funded by the TUM Institute for Advanced Study.
