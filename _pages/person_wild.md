**Dr.-Ing. Thomas Wild**  
Senior Researcher, Chair of Integrated Systems, TUM School of Computation, Information and
Technology

Thomas Wild works on hardware-assisted system optimization, power modeling and management for
multicore processors, and traffic steering in edge networks. At the Chair of Integrated Systems he
supervises theses and internships carried out in industry, and he is the EUROPRACTICE
representative for the TUM city campus.
