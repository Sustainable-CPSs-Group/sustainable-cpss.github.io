---
title: Sustainable Cyber-Physical Systems
description: Research on batteryless sensing, ultra low-power wireless networking, and harvesting-aware communication for industrial monitoring and maintenance.
---

We are a research group at the TUM Institute for Advanced Study. We study how sensing, ultra power-efficient wireless communication, and embedded artificial intelligence can be combined to monitor, buildings and infrastructure while using less energy and requiring less maintenance.

Our work includes the design of low power wireless communication protocols, harvesting system and AI-based, lightweight anomaly detection. The goals is to make devices batteryless, such that they become fully self-sustained by harvesting their energy from the surrounding. In such scenarios, the power supply becomes intermittent and many nodes share the same radio channels. Hence, machine-learning models and wireless communication protocols need to 1) operate with minimalistic power budgets and 2) support the execution of inference and communication tasks that are spread over multiple power cycles.

We are interested in how such a network could respond to a developing fault or an engineer’s question: collect more detail from the relevant sensors, prioritize urgent messages, and use AI to help interpret the evidence. This brings together low-power networking, predictable timing, and human expertise in industrial maintenance.
