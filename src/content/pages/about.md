---
title: Sustainable Cyber-Physical Systems
description: Research on energy efficiency, safety, and maintenance in cyber-physical systems.
---

We work on **sustainable cyber-physical systems**: the embedded computers and software that sense and control physical processes — machines on a production line, autonomous robots, vehicles, and instrumented infrastructure.

Modern systems combine machine learning, feedback control, networking, and real-time components. That composition consumes growing amounts of energy, needs constant monitoring and maintenance, and can be slow and costly to diagnose when it fails.

Our research examines how to design these systems so they are sustainable to **build**, **operate**, and **maintain**. We study energy-harvesting operation, safety guarantees for lightweight learning components, edge computing, and methods for detecting degradation and adapting system behaviour.
