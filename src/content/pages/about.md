---
title: Sustainable Cyber-Physical Systems
description: Research on energy efficiency, safety, and maintenance in cyber-physical systems.
---

We are a research group at the TUM Institute for Advanced Study. We study how cyber-physical systems can use less energy, operate safely, and require less maintenance.

These systems connect computing with the physical world: sensors monitor machines, software makes decisions, and controllers guide their operation. Our work focuses on the challenges that arise when sensing, communication, control, and machine learning must work together with limited resources.

We investigate batteryless devices powered by harvested energy, safe autonomy with small learning models, computation at the edge, and methods for detecting degradation. Across these areas, we consider what it takes to build, operate, and maintain a system over its lifetime.

The group is funded by a **Dieter Schwarz Courageous Research Grant** held by [Prof. Samarjit Chakraborty](https://cs.unc.edu/person/samarjit-chakraborty/). Our hosts at TUM are [Prof. Andreas Herkersdorf](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/lis/persons/management/andreas-herkersdorf/) and [Prof. Marco Caccamo](https://rtsl.cps.mw.tum.de/personal_page/mcaccamo/).
