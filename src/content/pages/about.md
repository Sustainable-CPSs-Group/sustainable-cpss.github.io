---
title: Sustainable Cyber-Physical Systems
description: Research on batteryless sensing, Bluetooth Low Energy, and adaptive wireless networks for industrial monitoring and maintenance.
---

We are a research group at the TUM Institute for Advanced Study. We study how sensing, wireless communication, and embedded intelligence can help monitor machines while using less energy and requiring less maintenance.

Our work includes Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) communication and lightweight anomaly detection. A longer-term goal is to connect dense networks of batteryless sensors powered by harvested energy. These devices need to report useful measurements even when power is intermittent and many nodes share the same radio channels.

We are interested in how such a network could respond to a developing fault or an engineer’s question: collect more detail from the relevant sensors, prioritise urgent messages, and use AI to help interpret the evidence. This brings together low-power networking, predictable timing, and human expertise in industrial maintenance.

The group is funded by a **Dieter Schwarz Courageous Research Grant** held by [Prof. Samarjit Chakraborty](https://cs.unc.edu/person/samarjit-chakraborty/). Our hosts at TUM are [Prof. Andreas Herkersdorf](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/lis/persons/management/andreas-herkersdorf/) and [Prof. Marco Caccamo](https://rtsl.cps.mw.tum.de/personal_page/mcaccamo/).
